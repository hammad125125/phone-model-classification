MobileNetV2 Mechanics & Transfer Learning

MobileNetV2 introduces inverted residual blocks and linear bottlenecks. Standard residual blocks expand features, compress them with convolutions, and add skip connections. In contrast, MobileNetV2 uses an expand → depthwise filter → project back inverted topology.

1. Depthwise Separable Convolutions

Splits standard convolution into a 3×3 depthwise spatial filter per channel followed by a 1×1 pointwise projection. Reduces computation by approximately 8× to 9× compared to standard convolutions with minimal accuracy drop.

2. Two-Stage Training Protocol

Phase 1: Freeze MobileNetV2 backbone; train custom classification head with Adam (lr=1e-3).
Phase 2: Unfreeze upper layers (from layer 100 onwards) and fine-tune with a conservative learning rate (lr=1e-5) while freezing BatchNormalization layers.
