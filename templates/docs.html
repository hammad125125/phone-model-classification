Comprehensive breakdown of problem formulation, engineering methodology, architectural decisions, and constraints.

🎯 Problem Statement

Smartphones share increasingly homogeneous industrial form factors: rectangular glass slabs, curved metallic edges, and minimal front bezels. Distinguishing exact phone models or brands visually requires recognizing subtle micro-features—such as camera island geometries, sensor alignments, notch dimensions, and flash positions. Developing a lightweight, edge-deployable deep learning classifier capable of robust categorization under variable lighting, angles, and phone cases is an open practical challenge.

📌 Semester Objectives

  • Curate and partition an image dataset into strictly disjoint training, validation, and testing splits.
  • Implement transfer learning leveraging ImageNet pretrained MobileNetV2 feature representations.
  • Design and optimize a task-specific classification head with regularized dense layers and dropout.
  • Formulate a reproducible training and evaluation pipeline with quantitative classification metrics.
  • Deploy a modular, production-ready web inference application and REST API.

🔬 Methodology & Engineering Pipeline

1. Preprocessing & Data Pipeline: Images are verified using PIL to eliminate corrupted headers and 0-byte artifacts. Each image is scaled to 224×224 resolution using bicubic interpolation and normalized to the range \([-1, 1]\). Training datasets undergo data augmentation (random flips, rotations of ±12%, zooms of ±10%, and contrast adjustments) to prevent overfitting.

2. Backbone Network: MobileNetV2 was chosen over ResNet-50 and VGG-16 due to its parameter efficiency (3.4M vs 25M+ parameters) and rapid inference time (<50ms on CPU). Its depthwise separable convolutions separate spatial filtering from feature combination.

3. Transfer Learning Protocol: During Phase 1, the pretrained backbone is frozen to prevent gradient explosion from corrupting early visual representations. The custom classification head (GlobalAveragePooling2D → BatchNorm → Dropout(0.3) → Dense(128, ReLU) → Dropout(0.2) → Softmax) is trained with Adam (\(\alpha = 10^{-3}\)). In Phase 2, upper residual blocks (layer index ≥ 100) are unlocked and fine-tuned with a lower learning rate (\(\alpha = 10^{-5}\)).

⚠️ Current Limitations

  • Opaque protective phone cases or heavy skins obscure rear chassis geometry and brand logos.
  • Sub-model differentiation (e.g., iPhone 13 vs iPhone 14 standard editions) is constrained when camera island diagonal layouts are nearly identical without millimeter-level scale references.
  • Severe specular reflections on glossy glass backs can introduce classification noise.

🚀 Future Work & Extensions

  • Implement multi-view classification fusing front, back, and side profile images simultaneously.
  • Integrate an attention-based Vision Transformer (ViT) or YOLO-based camera module bounding box detector for localized feature extraction.
  • Quantize model to TensorFlow Lite (INT8 / FP16) for real-time offline inference on Android and iOS devices.
