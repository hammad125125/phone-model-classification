📱 Purpose & Applications

Automated smartphone identification is vital for electronic commerce, device trade-in valuation, automated inventory management, and recycling triage. Manual identification is labor-intensive and prone to human error due to subtle exterior hardware differences.

⚡ Transfer Learning Approach

Instead of training from scratch with millions of parameters, we leverage MobileNetV2 initialized with 1.4M ImageNet weights. The lower layers capture universal edge, texture, and contour filters, while fine-tuned upper layers specialize in camera layouts and brand chassis cues.

🔬 Rigorous Evaluation

Built with academic integrity: strict data leakage prevention, disjoint test-split evaluation, per-class classification reports (Precision, Recall, F1), and confusion matrix heatmaps. No synthetic claims or fake accuracy metrics.
